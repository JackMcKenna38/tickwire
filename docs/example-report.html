Assumptions

  • Strategy: quote one bid and one ask 3.00 bp either side of the mid, shifted 1.00 bp against inventory per order held; $1000 per order; at most 5 orders of inventory; requote when the target moves 1 tick(s). Post-only.
  • Latency: 100 ms each way, for new orders, cancels and fill reports alike. An order being replaced can still be filled until the cancel arrives.
  • Queue: a new order joins behind all quantity displayed at its price. Trades at that price are taken from the front of the queue.
  • Cancellations (prorata): a drop in displayed quantity not explained by trades is assumed to be spread evenly across the level, so the queue ahead shrinks in proportion.
  • Fees: Kraken Pro spot maker fee of 0.40% (published schedule, retrieved 2026-11-14), charged on every honest fill.
  • No market impact: the recorded market does not react to our orders. Both models share this limitation; it flatters both.
  • This is a simulation on public market data. No orders were placed.
